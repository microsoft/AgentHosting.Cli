# 14039: Deployment channel is not configured

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

The channel you tried to show or update does not exist on the selected deployment's Azure bot.

## Why this happens

Knowing a supported channel type does not mean the channel is enabled on every bot. The deployment selector may point to a different bot, the channel may never have been configured, or it may have been removed.

## How to fix it

Inspect the configured channels:

```text
msagent deployment channel list --deployment-id <deployment-id>
```

Correct the deployment or channel selector if necessary. If the desired channel is absent, use `msagent deployment channel create` with the correct `--channel` and required parameters.

Inspect `msagent deployment channel create --channel <channel-name> --help` for the channel-specific parameter schema. Do not recreate the entire bot or delete another channel to resolve a missing-channel lookup.

## How to avoid it

List configured channels before updating them and keep deployment IDs in your channel configuration instructions.
