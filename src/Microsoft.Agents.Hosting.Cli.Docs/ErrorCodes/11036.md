# 11036: Connections were bound but workflows were not activated

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

The connection bindings were saved, but activation of workflows depending on them failed. The agent is not ready until those workflows are active.

## Why this happens

Saving a binding and enabling dependent workflows are separate operations. The environment may reject activation because of permissions, workflow requirements, or a service problem. The error includes the activation failure.

## How to fix it

Keep the existing bindings; they do not need to be declared again.

Resolve the reported activation problem, then rerun `msagent deployment connection update` with the same deployment selection to retry workflow activation.

Verify that the dependent workflows are active before treating the agent as usable. Do not recreate references or delete workflows simply because activation did not finish.

## How to avoid it

Check workflow activation permissions and target-environment requirements in addition to connection readiness. Verify the complete update result, not just that bindings were saved.
