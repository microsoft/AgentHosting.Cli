# 16006: Requested bot SKU or quota is unavailable

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

Provisioning could not obtain the requested Azure Bot Service SKU or capacity in the target context.

## Why this happens

SKU availability and quota can constrain creation even when the account, resource group, and resource name are valid. ABS preview marks SKU quota as unknown because it cannot prove capacity through a non-mutating check. A clean preview is not a reservation of capacity.

## How to fix it

Check the reported Azure reason, requested SKU, subscription, and location. Ask the subscription administrator to review quota or availability.

The supported bot SKUs are `F0` and `S1`. If the free SKU's capacity is the issue, consider `S1` only after confirming cost and organizational approval. Preview the revised request before applying.

Inspect existing Azure state after a failed mutation before provisioning under another name.

## How to avoid it

Check capacity and billing constraints during environment preparation. Do not assume preview can guarantee a later write will satisfy quota.
