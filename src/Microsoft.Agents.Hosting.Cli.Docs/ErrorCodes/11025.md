# 11025: Sharing cannot change the owner's access

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

The selected person owns the agent. Their owner access cannot be granted, downgraded, or revoked through sharing.

## Why this happens

Ownership and ordinary sharing are different access relationships. Sharing commands manage other principals' use or edit access; they do not transfer ownership or remove an owner's rights.

## How to fix it

If the intended recipient is someone else, correct `--user` and verify the identity before retrying.

If ownership really needs to change, use the ownership-transfer workflow in Copilot Studio with the appropriate authorized account. Review the resulting owner and access state afterward.

Do not try repeated share or unshare operations to remove the owner's rights.

## How to avoid it

Review `msagent agent share show` before access changes and distinguish owner transfers from ordinary grants and revocations.
