# 19023: Playground cleanup failed

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

The CLI could not confirm safe cleanup of the owned playground process.

## Why this happens

The launcher's bounded process cleanup failed.

## How to fix it

Inspect the specific playground window and owned process. Stop only the intended session and verify cleanup before retrying.

## How to avoid it

Close playground normally and avoid terminating the parent abruptly. Do not kill unrelated processes by name.
