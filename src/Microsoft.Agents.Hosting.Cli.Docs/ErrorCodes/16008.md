# 16008: Provisioning access or preflight check failed

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

Provisioning was blocked by insufficient access or another failed preflight check.

## Why this happens

ABS apply checks subscription and resource-group visibility and refuses a plan with blocking issues. The account may lack read access to those scopes, or another preflight may have failed. Actual write permission cannot be proven by preview; Azure validates it when the write is attempted.

## How to fix it

Read the named failed check rather than assuming every failure needs a broader role. Confirm the correct signed-in account, tenant, subscription, and resource group.

For scope access, ask an administrator for the least privilege needed: the documented ABS guidance is at least Reader for preflight and Contributor for apply at the appropriate scope. For another failed check, resolve that specific condition.

Rerun `msagent abs preview` and inspect the resulting plan before applying. The `abs` group requires `AzureBotServiceFeatures`; preview never mutates Azure.

## How to avoid it

Verify scope access in advance and keep read access, write permission, provider registration, and policy checks distinct.
