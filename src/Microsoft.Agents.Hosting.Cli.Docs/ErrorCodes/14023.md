# 14023: Package upload success could not be confirmed

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

The upload response did not provide the required successful operation and Package Revision identifiers. The CLI cannot confirm the upload as complete.

## Why this happens

The upload result must report status `Succeeded` and valid non-empty operation and revision IDs. This check protects you from proceeding with an incomplete or incompatible service response. The upload may already have persisted remotely even though its response cannot be accepted.

## How to fix it

Do not use an unconfirmed revision for deployment. Preserve the reported request IDs and ask the service owner to inspect the upload operation and stored package. Confirm that the service implements the expected v1 terminal upload response.

Decide whether another upload is needed only after checking remote state. Repeating a request is not a reliable fix for a response-contract problem.

## How to avoid it

Use compatible CLI and AMS deployments. Keep the original artifact unchanged while investigating, so its digest and size remain available for comparison.
