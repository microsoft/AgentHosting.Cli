# 12001: Authentication failed

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

The CLI could not complete authentication or use the selected credential. The accompanying safe
message identifies the particular failure without exposing credential or provider response content.

## Why this happens

The credential may be unavailable, expired or rejected; a PFX may be unreadable or lack its private
key; federation runtime configuration/trust may be missing or invalid. A pinned Azure source may
fail and is not silently replaced. Retained embedded account selection can also fail ambiguously.
CLI selector/mode validation uses usage 99002 where appropriate, not this general code.

## How to fix it

Use `msagent auth list` and `msagent auth who` to inspect token-free metadata. Check the intended
tenant/application/cloud and configured credential method. Restore the same pinned source or
explicitly create the intended identity with `auth create`; do not silently substitute identities.
For federation, verify matching tenant/application trust and the Actions
`ACTIONS_ID_TOKEN_REQUEST_URL`/`ACTIONS_ID_TOKEN_REQUEST_TOKEN` or Azure DevOps
`MSAGENT_ADO_ID_TOKEN_REQUEST_URL`/`MSAGENT_ADO_ID_TOKEN_REQUEST_TOKEN` runtime values.
Follow any safe method-specific remediation. Inspect committed/cleanup guidance before another mutation.

Do not share access tokens or token-cache contents when reporting a persistent problem.

## How to avoid it

Use an unambiguous named profile or fresh list index, protect credential configuration, and verify
the selected identity/cloud before resource-changing commands. See [authentication](../Command_Auth.md).
