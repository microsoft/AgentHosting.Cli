# 13005: The environment and account tenants do not match

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

The CLI refused to select or use an environment whose tenant does not match the active account's tenant binding.

## Why this happens

You may have switched accounts while retaining an old environment selection. The environment's tenant metadata may also be missing or inconsistent.

## How to fix it

Check `msagent auth status` and the tenant IDs printed in the error. For the intended environment, sign in to its tenant with `msagent auth login --tenant <tenant-id>`, then refresh its selection using `msagent env select <environment-id>`.

If you intended to stay in the current tenant, use `msagent env list` to choose its correct environment instead. Follow the printed remediation when the environment's tenant is unknown.

## How to avoid it

Verify environment context after account switches. Treat account and environment as a matched pair rather than independent settings that can safely be reused across tenants.
