# 17028: A removed connection reference still exists in the cloud

[All error codes](../ErrorCodes.md)

## What happened

The local connection-reference declaration was removed, but deleting its Dataverse connection-reference record failed.

## Why this happens

Another component may still depend on the record, or the read/delete request may fail because of access or service problems. The underlying diagnostic identifies what prevented retirement.

## How to fix it

Inspect the named reference and its dependent actions or workflows in the destination. Remove or rebind dependencies only when that is the intended design, and resolve any permission or service failure.

The local and cloud states now differ; do not assume deletion completed. Reinspect them before retrying the supported reference-removal operation. Do not delete a shared underlying connection simply to force a reference to disappear.

## How to avoid it

Review dependencies before retiring references. Keep a recoverable copy of declarations and use supported connection-management commands rather than manually changing synchronization caches.
